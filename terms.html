Terms and conditions

Middle Coast Software Inc. is conducting a limited text messaging campaign to test a software integration as part of a development effort. The campaign is intended to end on Thanksgiving Day, November 26, 2026. Messages are for testing, not marketing.

Participation

Participating developers gave verbal consent at the event kickoff meeting to receive test messages. Message frequency varies according to the development effort during the test period. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to a campaign text, telling us in person during the event, or emailing contact@middlecoastsoftware.com. Reply HELP for help or email us at the same address.

Delivery

Message delivery depends on your mobile carrier and may be delayed or unavailable.

Privacy

Information about the test campaign and how participant information is used and shared is available in our privacy policy.
