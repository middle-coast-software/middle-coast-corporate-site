Privacy policy

This policy applies to Middle Coast Software Inc.'s limited text messaging campaign for testing a software integration. The campaign is part of a development effort and is intended to end on Thanksgiving Day, November 26, 2026.

Information and use

To conduct the test, we use participants' phone numbers, the messages exchanged, and information associated with message delivery. We use this information for the development and testing effort, not for marketing.

Information sharing

Twilio and mobile carriers process phone numbers and message information as needed to deliver the texts. We do not otherwise share personal information with third parties or sell it. Mobile opt-in information and consent are not shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Participation and choices

Participating developers gave verbal consent at the event kickoff meeting. You may withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to a campaign text, telling us in person during the event, or emailing contact@middlecoastsoftware.com. Reply HELP for help or contact us at the same email address.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your information, email contact@middlecoastsoftware.com.
